Frequently Asked Questions

What does Camera Stealth Mode do? +
When social platforms (LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Meta) analyze an upload, completely empty metadata can sometimes trigger secondary algorithmic checks. Camera Stealth Mode strips all AI manifests (C2PA, JUMBF, Prompts) and injects genuine camera hardware EXIF tags (Sony, Canon, Apple, Nikon) with realistic lens, shutter speed, ISO, and focal length values.
How does the Analog Humanizer counter SynthID and AI classifiers? +
Pixel-level invisible watermarks (like Google SynthID) and AI classifiers (Hive, Sightengine) operate by detecting mathematical periodicity and unnaturally smooth gradients inherent in latent diffusion models. The Analog Humanizer applies subtle, film-like micro-sensor dithering across the pixel matrix. This disrupts the high-frequency harmonics that AI detectors search for, while remaining virtually invisible to the human eye.
Why do LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok label my photos as "Created with AI"? +
When AI models like Google Gemini, Midjourney, DALL-E, or Canva generate photos, they insert hidden metadata tags such as C2PA Content Credentials manifests (APP11/JUMBF) or IPTC digitalSourceType markers. When you upload your media, social platforms scan these tags and attach an AI badge. Our tool strips these specific markers in 1 millisecond.
Does stripping metadata reduce image or video quality? +
No! Our metadata stripper operates directly at the binary byte stream level. It removes metadata segments without decoding and re-compressing the visual entropy data. Your JPEG, PNG, or WebP pixels remain 100% bit-for-bit identical.
Are my files uploaded to any server? +
No. All algorithms execute 100% locally on your machine inside your browser. You can even run this website entirely offline without an active internet connection.
