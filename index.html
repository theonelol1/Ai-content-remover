Frequently Asked Questions (AEO / FAQ)

Direct answers to the most common questions about C2PA metadata, social AI badges, watermark removal, and media privacy.

What is C2PA metadata and why do social platforms use it to label AI photos? +

C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity) is an open technical standard that embeds cryptographic manifests into image and video files (such as APP11 segments in JPEG or c2pa chunks in PNG). AI generators including Midjourney, Google Gemini, DALL-E 3, and Canva automatically attach these manifests. When you upload media, platforms like Instagram, Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok inspect file headers and automatically display an "AI info" or "Made with AI" badge if C2PA markers are found.

How do I remove the "Made with AI" badge on Instagram and LinkedIn? +

To eliminate the automated "Made with AI" badge, you must remove both the C2PA JUMBF manifest and the IPTC trainedAlgorithmicMedia metadata tag. PurePixel Studio strips these signatures instantly without re-encoding your image, and then applies Camera Stealth Mode to inject authentic digital camera EXIF (like a Sony Alpha 7 IV or Canon EOS R5). Social platforms then register the file as a standard hardware photograph, bypassing automated AI tagging.

What does Camera Stealth Mode do and why is it necessary? +

When you strip all metadata from an image, completely blank EXIF headers can trigger secondary heuristics on social networks. Camera Stealth Mode solves this by populating stripped files with authentic hardware EXIF tags—including realistic shutter speed (1/250s), ISO (100), aperture (f/2.8), focal length, and camera manufacturer data (Sony, Canon, Apple, Nikon, Fujifilm)—ensuring your files appear as genuine camera captures.

How does mathematical reverse alpha unblending remove Gemini watermarks without blur? +

Traditional watermark removers use generative AI inpainting, which hallucinates blurred or smudged textures. PurePixel Studio uses exact inverse color blending based on the known alpha compositing equation: Watermarked = (1 - α) · Original + α · Logo. By applying calibrated D4 dihedral group alpha matrices, our engine algebraically solves for the original pixel values: Original = (Watermarked - α · Logo) / (1 - α). This recovers 100% of the underlying background details with zero blur and zero hallucination.

Can PurePixel Studio remove watermarks from Veo AI videos? +

Yes! PurePixel features a dedicated Veo & Video Watermark engine. It performs frame-by-frame canvas processing locally in your browser for MP4, WebM, and MOV files. Unlike other video converters that drop or desync audio tracks, PurePixel captures and routes original audio streams via the Web Audio API, guaranteeing 100% audio fidelity and lip-sync preservation.

How does the Anti-AI Analog Humanizer counter Google SynthID? +

Invisible watermarking systems like Google SynthID and neural AI classifiers detect mathematical periodicity, unnatural gradient smoothness, and latent diffusion frequency harmonics. The Anti-AI Analog Humanizer introduces subtle, organic photographic film-grain micro-dithering across high-frequency color channels. This breaks the mathematical periodicity that classifiers search for, while remaining visually imperceptible to the human eye.

Does stripping metadata reduce image or video quality? +

No. PurePixel Studio operates directly at the binary byte stream level. It removes metadata segments without decoding and re-compressing the visual entropy data. Your JPEG, PNG, or WebP pixels remain 100% bit-for-bit identical with zero resolution loss, zero color shift, and zero compression artifacts.

Are my files uploaded to any server or cloud database? +

No. PurePixel Studio is built on a 100% private, client-side zero-knowledge architecture. Every operation executes in your browser's local sandbox using WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and JavaScript ArrayBuffers. No files, image buffers, or media metadata are ever uploaded to any server. You can even run PurePixel completely offline with Wi-Fi disconnected.
